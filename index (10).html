FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Where does the campaign money come from?

From your coin's pump.fun creator fees. Every buy and sell pays a small creator fee. At launch, Bond points that fee at a campaign vault, split the way you choose between UGC creators, marketing, and your own wallet.

How do creators get paid?

Creators join a campaign, post with your coin's tag or link, and submit the post. Views are verified, then the vault pays out at the rate you set per 1,000 views, in SOL, straight to their wallet.

Can I change the split later?

You set the split at launch. Locking it means holders and creators can trust the budget will be there, and it's what makes a Bond coin different from a dev wallet promising to "do marketing".

What if the coin graduates from the bonding curve?

Campaigns keep running. Creator fees keep flowing into the vault after graduation, and so do creator payouts.

I'm a creator. How do I join?

Connect a wallet, link your TikTok, X or YouTube account, and browse live campaigns. No follower minimum. You're paid on views, so small accounts that go viral earn the same rate as big ones.
